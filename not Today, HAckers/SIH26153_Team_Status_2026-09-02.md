### 4. Known Limitations (Documented)

Singleton Attack Types (not LOEO-evaluated):
- DDOS-HOIC (1 episode, 8 windows)
- Infiltration-Compromise (1 episode, 97 windows)
- Infiltration-Portscan (1 episode, 58 windows)

Statement: These types have exactly one contiguous episode in the dataset. Cross-episode generalization cannot be tested for them. They are flagged as dataset limitations in VALIDATION_REPORT.md.

Schedule Signal Risk (Phase 2 Mandatory Gate):
The LSTM/GRU + GraphSAGE model evaluation must demonstrate it outperforms a schedule-only baseline on held-out episodes (LOEO), not just on IID window samples.
