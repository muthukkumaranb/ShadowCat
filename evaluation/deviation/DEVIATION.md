Generated by `evaluation/deviation/deviation_eval.py` from `data-engineering/data/ucs/ucs_windows.parquet`; all numbers are in `deviation_results.json`.

Deviation = mean |actual next PCA state - world-model predicted mean| (the procedure of `ml1/scripts/generate_loeo_deviation.py`, epochs = 2). Scored against each window's label_binary.
